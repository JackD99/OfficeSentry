Security

It holds read access to many clients, so it does as little as possible with it

Read the security model
  • Read-only in your clients' tenants. The app that reads them asks only for read permissions. The code has no way to write to Microsoft Graph, and only runs Exchange commands that start with Get-.
  • Sending email is separate and optional. Reports and alerts go out through your own mail server, or through a separate app in your own Microsoft 365 tenant that may send mail and nothing else, limited to one mailbox. It has no access to your clients.
  • Certificate sign-in, no stored passwords. The app signs in with a certificate whose private key is encrypted in the database. Secrets and private keys can never be downloaded.
  • Every client kept apart. People see only the clients they're given, and only what their role opens there. Clients see only their own reports, and when they sign in with Microsoft, only from their own organisation's tenant.
  • Your team signs in your way. Staff sign in with a password and a code from an authenticator app, or with their Microsoft work account under your own Conditional Access and MFA, through a sign-in app in your tenant that has no access to anything else.
  • Runs on your own server. Your clients' data stays with you. There's no telemetry: nothing is sent to us.
  • Releases you can check. Each release image carries a signed record of how it was built, so you can check it came from this project's code before you run it. The Update button checks it before it installs anything.

Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. Office Sentry is free software under the AGPL-3.0. You run it on your own server; a small cloud server is enough for most MSPs.

{% if not source_public %}
Can I see the source code?

Not yet. The code and its install image stay private while security and reliability testing finishes, and the source is published under the AGPL-3.0 after that.{% if contact %} Until then, ask for early access to try it with your own clients.{% endif %}

{% endif %}
Can it change anything in a client's tenant?

No. It only asks your clients' tenants for read permissions, and its code can't write to Microsoft Graph or run Exchange commands other than ones that read. The one thing it sends is your own email: reports and alerts, through your mail server or a separate app in your own tenant that can only send mail.

Does it read anyone's email?

No. It reads settings, mailbox details such as size, forwarding and permissions, and inbox rules, never message contents. What Office Sentry reads lists every permission, written for your clients.

Can my team sign in with their Microsoft accounts?

Yes. Office Sentry creates a small sign-in app in your own tenant, with no access to anything else, and your staff sign in under your Conditional Access and MFA. Their role comes from Entra at every sign-in; which clients they see is still set in Office Sentry. Staff sign-in has the steps.

Can my clients sign in with their own Microsoft accounts?

Yes. Add each client's contacts by their work address and they sign in with the account they already use, under their own organisation's MFA, from their own company's tenant only. Nobody is invited into your tenant as a guest, and they only ever see their own company's reports. Client sign-in has the steps.

How do I update it?

Settings → Updates lists every newer release with its notes and anything to do first. Turn on the optional updater and its Update button installs a release, after checking it's genuine, and goes back to the old version by itself if the new one doesn't start. Updating by hand with Docker works as well. Updating has both.

Where is the data kept?

On your own server, in one database you can back up and restore. Office Sentry has no telemetry, so nothing is sent to us.

How is it different from Microsoft's own admin centres?

Microsoft shows one tenant at a time, in its own words, with little history. Office Sentry keeps every night's results for every client, compares them month by month and across clients, and turns them into branded reports.
