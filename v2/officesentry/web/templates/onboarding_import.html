One row per client, with column names on the first row. Excel's Save as CSV works.

  • name: how the client appears in Office Sentry and on reports.
  • tenant: the Directory (tenant) ID, or any domain the tenant uses, such as contoso.onmicrosoft.com.
  • group (optional): a client group; several separated by ;. Missing groups are created.
  • contact (optional): who to send the consent link to, as jane@contoso.com or Jane Smith <jane@contoso.com>. Also added to the client's email contacts.
  • cloud (optional): leave empty for Commercial; or GCC High, DoD or China.

Up to {{ '{:,}'.format(1000) }} rows at a time. Clients already in Office Sentry are skipped, not changed.
