{{ brand.display_name }} would like to add a report on who deleted, downloaded and shared files, and who opened other people's mailboxes, to your Microsoft 365 reports. That comes from your Microsoft 365 audit log, which the app you approved before can't read. So this is a second app, with one read-only permission. A Global Administrator of your Microsoft 365 approves it once; it takes a couple of minutes.

The app connects with a certificate {{ brand.display_name }} holds, not with anyone's password. Once you've approved it, {{ brand.display_name }} asks Microsoft to make your new audit records available to it (and, if the add-on is switched off, to stop). That only decides whether the app may read the records; it changes nothing in your tenant.

Before you approve

Microsoft only records this activity while auditing is on, which it is by default. If someone turned it off, the reports stay empty until it's on again: in the Microsoft Purview portal, go to Audit and choose Start recording user and admin activity.

Removing it

You can remove it at any time, and it stops straight away. In the Microsoft Entra admin centre ({{ cloud.admin_centre.split('//')[-1] }}), go to Enterprise applications, open {{ app }}, choose Properties, then Delete. Your other reports carry on as before.
