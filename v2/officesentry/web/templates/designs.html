How it works

  • Every block draws on data the portal already has: the monthly review, the checks, the detail reports, licences and this month's commentary.
  • A block with nothing to show for a client is left out of their report, so one custom report fits every client.
  • Each client's Reports tab lists every custom report on that client's data, as a page and a PDF.
  • To send one, tick it in {{ page_link(url_for('reporting.plans'), 'a report plan') }} or a client's email schedule, or choose Send on a schedule beside it on the client's Custom reports page. It goes as a PDF.
