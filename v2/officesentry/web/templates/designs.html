How it works

  • Every block draws on data the portal already has: the monthly review, the checks, the detail reports, licences and this month's commentary.
  • A block with nothing to show for a client is left out of their report, so one design fits every client.
  • To send a design, tick it in a report plan (Month-end → Report plans) or a client's email schedule. It goes as a PDF.
