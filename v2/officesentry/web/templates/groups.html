How groups work

  • A client can be in as many groups as you like.
  • Pick a group at the top of Clients, What changed, Month-end and Client settings to see only those clients. Downloads follow the group you picked.
  • A digest can cover one group instead of every client.
  • Groups never show anyone a client they can't already see. To let an analyst see a whole group, including clients added to it later, tick the group on their page under {{ page_link(url_for('admin.users'), 'Users') }}.
