Licence prices

What your clients pay for each licence per month. Licence waste, right-sizing savings and the Accounts page put money on licences with these, for each client at its margin.

How prices are worked out

A licence's price is your own price for it when you've entered one, else Microsoft's list price, plus your margin: what your reseller adds and what you add, in one percentage.
