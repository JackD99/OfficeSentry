How roles work

  • A role is a set of capabilities (what someone may do) and a scope (every client, or the clients and groups granted on their page).
  • Built-in roles can be changed and put back to their defaults. Administrator always holds everything, so there is always someone who can fix things.
  • On a person's page you can add or take away single capabilities for them, everywhere or on one client. Taking away always wins.
  • Changing a role signs out everyone holding it, so they pick up the change when they next sign in.
