Whatever your reseller exports: the columns don't have to be in any order, and the headings on the first row say which is which.

  • A column of product names or part numbers (Product, Description, SKU, Offer). Names are matched as written, with wording like "NCE" or "Annual commitment" ignored.
  • A column of prices (Price, Unit price, Cost). A "list price" column is only used when there's no column of what you pay.
  • Prices are what one user costs you for a month. If they're a year's, say so on the next page and they're divided by 12.

Only licences Office Sentry reports on are read; everything else in the list is left out. Your margin is added on top of these, so clients still see what they pay.
