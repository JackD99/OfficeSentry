Offboarding removes {{ tenant.name }} from Office Sentry for good. Work through the steps, then delete. If the client might come back, archive it instead: that hides it and keeps its data.

  1. Download a final copy
    Keep the last reports for your records, or send them to the client. Once deleted, they can't be made again.
    {{ downloads([('Monthly review PDF', url_for('reports.tenant_report_pdf', tenant_id=tenant.id), ''), ('Monthly review XLSX', url_for('reports.tenant_report_xlsx', tenant_id=tenant.id), ''), ('Cyber insurance evidence PDF', url_for('evidence.scheme_pdf', tenant_id=tenant.id, scheme='insurance'), ''), ('Cyber insurance evidence XLSX', url_for('evidence.scheme_xlsx', tenant_id=tenant.id, scheme='insurance'), '')]) }}

    Detail reports download from their own pages under Detail reports.

  2. Remove Office Sentry from {{ tenant.name }}'s Microsoft 365
    Office Sentry can't do this itself: it only ever reads the tenant. A Global Administrator of {{ tenant.name }} (you, or the client) does it in the Microsoft Entra admin centre:
    1. Roles and admins → Global Reader → select {{ app }} → Remove assignments{% if r.roles_checked and not r.global_reader %} (it didn't hold the role at the last check){% endif %}.
    2. Enterprise applications → {{ app }} → Properties → Delete. This withdraws the consent; Office Sentry can't sign in to the tenant again.
    Text to send the client

    The same steps are in What Office Sentry reads (docs/what-office-sentry-reads.md, "How to remove access"), the document you sent when onboarding.
