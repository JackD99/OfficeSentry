# Roadmap

Office Sentry is a read-only Microsoft 365 reporting tool for MSPs. This page says what's
planned, roughly in order. It changes as we learn; ideas and questions are welcome in
Discussions.

## Now

- Reliability for self-hosters: restore, health checks and alerts that tell the truth.
- "Clients" wording, and a shorter monthly review with an appendix.
- Proven sizing: measured page and collection times at 200 client tenants.

## Next

- Teams: private and shared channels and their guests, inactive teams, installed apps.
- Mail flow: delivered, quarantined and failed mail per domain, top spam and phishing
  recipients. Comes with one approval request per client.
- Audit log add-on (optional, separate app): who deleted, downloaded or shared files, and
  who opened someone else's mailbox.

## Later

- CIS Microsoft 365 and Essential Eight evidence packs.
- Pages for a mailbox, a group or team, and a SharePoint site.
- Local date and currency formats in client reports.

## Not planned

Office Sentry never writes to a client tenant. Management actions, bulk changes,
remediation, reading mail or chat content, an on-premises agent and GDAP management are
out of scope.
