# Roadmap

Office Sentry is a read-only Microsoft 365 reporting tool for MSPs. This page says what's
planned, roughly in order. It changes as we learn; ideas and questions are welcome in
Discussions.

What has shipped is in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). 2.2 brought a page for every mailbox,
group and site; the Teams, Site access, Site permissions, File and folder permissions, Mail
flow, Administrative units and Licence changes reports; Cyber Essentials, cyber insurance and
supplier questionnaires judged from the data; evidence packs for IASME Cyber Assurance, ISO
27001, UK GDPR, CIS Microsoft 365, NIST CSF 2.0, SOC 2 and the Essential Eight; and roles and
staff sign-in with Microsoft. 2.3 brought client sign-in with Microsoft; an Update button that
installs a release from the portal; a month-end review that leaves things out of one client's
report; the Intune policies and Mail rules and protection reports, BitLocker recovery keys and
PIM for groups; and projects you'd put on a quote. 2.4 brought the optional audit log add-on
and a check that each client's audit log is on; the Security alerts, Service health, Installed
apps and Trusted organisations and locations reports; Windows LAPS on the Devices report; a
new look, with light and dark for each person; and Switch client and search from any page.

## Next

Nothing is fixed yet. Ideas are welcome in Discussions.

## Later

- Local date and currency formats in client reports.

## Not planned

Office Sentry never writes to a client tenant. Management actions, bulk changes,
remediation, reading mail or chat content, an on-premises agent and GDAP management are
out of scope.
