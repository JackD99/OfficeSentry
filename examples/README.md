# Example reports

Real exports from Office Sentry, made from **fictional demo data**. Harbour IT is a made-up MSP and
its clients (Fabrikam Dental Group, Northwind Traders, Contoso Legal LLP, Kingsley Optometry) are
made-up companies; every person, address and `.example` domain is invented. Nothing here comes from
a real Microsoft 365 tenant. The single-client reports are for Fabrikam Dental Group.

Regenerate them from `v2/` with `python scripts/screenshots.py` after report changes.
